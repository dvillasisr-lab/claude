Ride With FAQ

Full rules
When does my name go on?

After the crew checks it. Once it's on the tractor, we email you a photo of your name on The Evil One.

What names get rejected?

Anything offensive or hateful, brand or business names, and full names of kids under 13 (first names or nicknames are fine). If we reject a name, you get a full refund. Businesses can see Sponsors.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Type their name for the panel and check out as usual. The photo of the name on the tractor goes to your email, so you can pass it on.

Is it tax-deductible?

No. Ride With is a sponsorship of a race team, not a donation to a charity.
