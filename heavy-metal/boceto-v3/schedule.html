First time at a pull?

  1. What is Pro Stock

    A diesel class with limits on engine size and turbo. The Evil One runs a Cat 3208 V8 at 680 CI and 10,000 lb.

  2. What is a full pull

    The sled gets harder to drag the farther it goes. Reach the end of the track and it's a full pull.

  3. Arrive early

    The grandstands fill up before the first class. Come early for a good seat and a look at the pits.

  4. Bring ear protection

    Pro Stock tractors are loud. Ear plugs or muffs for everyone, kids first.
