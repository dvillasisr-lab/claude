01 · 2014 to 2020

Twelve years in the making

It started in Tomah, Wisconsin, with one idea. Chris had always been a heavy metal fan, so when he decided to build a pulling tractor, the name was never up for debate.

The first bolt went in in February 2020. Then came nights and weekends stolen from work and family, sketches redrawn more times than anyone can count, and a shop in Waterloo that the whole family turned into a second home. Building Heavy Metal was never a solo job.

02 · 2026

The night it fired

In 2026 the moment came. Chris stood in the shop and turned the engine over for the first time.

At that exact moment, lightning struck, and the power went out.

Some would call it a coincidence. We call it a sign. From that night on, there was no doubt about what we had built.

La cita de Chris con groserías ("This thing is f#cking evil") se dejó fuera hasta que la dueña la apruebe para esta página.

03 · 2026 to today

The first pull

The first pull didn't go the way anyone hoped. A small, unexpected issue came up and the run ended before it should have. We won't sugarcoat it.

But getting Heavy Metal onto a real track at all, after twelve years of building it with our own hands, was already something to be proud of. The pull didn't finish that day. The story was just getting started.

This season we're chasing our first full pull: about 20 competitions on the NTPA and PPL Badger State circuits. No exhibitions. Every time it hits the track, it's there to pull.
