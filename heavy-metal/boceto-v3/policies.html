Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Heavy Metal Pro Stock ("Heavy Metal", "we", "us") collects through this store, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Heavy Metal Pro Stock is a tractor pulling team based in Waterloo, Wisconsin, USA. This store sells team merch that is printed to order, the "Ride With The Evil One" name program, and sponsorship information. Our store runs on Shopify.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Order details: name, email, phone, shipping and billing address, items purchased.
  • Hook Alerts sign ups: email address and, if you opt in to texts, your mobile number.
  • Ride With The Evil One: the name you submit for the tractor panel and your order details.
  • Sponsorship and contact requests: your name, company, email, phone and message.
  • Account information if you sign in to a customer account.

Information collected automatically: device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, referring site and how you use the store, collected with cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).

Payment information is processed by Shopify and its payment partners. We never see or store your full card number.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, print, ship and support your orders, including returns and replacements.
  • To send Hook Alerts: new merch drops and a heads up before each pull, by email and, only if you opted in, by text.
  • To review, print and place your Ride With name, and to send you the photo proof.
  • To answer sponsorship requests and send the media kit you asked for.
  • To keep the store secure, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To understand how the store is used and, only with your consent where required, to measure ads.

4. Who we share it with

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and processes payments.
  • Our print on demand partner (Printly), which receives your name, shipping address and the items ordered so it can print and ship them.
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your order and provide tracking.
  • Our email and SMS provider [name pending], to send Hook Alerts.
  • Analytics and advertising partners, only if you allow analytics or marketing cookies.
  • Authorities or others when required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not share your mobile number or text consent with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Sale and sharing under state law

If you allow marketing cookies, some ad tools may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information for cross context behavioral advertising under California and other state laws. You can opt out at any time on Your Privacy Choices. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a valid opt out.

6. Cookies

We use necessary cookies to run the store (cart, checkout, security). Analytics and marketing cookies are optional. Change your choice anytime in Cookie Preferences.

7. Your California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose.
  • Delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights.

We do not collect sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit. To make a request, email us (see section 11). We will verify your request by matching it to information we already have, and you may use an authorized agent. Residents of other states with privacy laws can make the same requests.

8. Retention

We keep order records as long as needed for taxes, accounting and warranty claims. Hook Alerts data is kept until you unsubscribe. Sponsorship requests are kept while we are in contact and then deleted.

9. Children

This store is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Security and changes

We use Shopify's security safeguards, but no method of transmission is 100% secure. We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.

11. Contact

Heavy Metal Pro Stock, Waterloo, WI. Email: [email pending]. Mailing address: [address pending].

Ride With Rules

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Ride With The Evil One puts your name on a panel on the tractor for the 2026 pulling season. Here is how it works.

Season
2026
Max length
24 characters
Proof
Photo emailed once it is on

1. What you get

Your name printed on the supporters panel of The Evil One for the 2026 season, and a photo of the panel with your name once it is on the tractor. This is a service: nothing ships.

2. Name rules

  • Up to 24 characters, including spaces. Letters, numbers, spaces and . & ' - only.
  • No hate speech, slurs, threats, profanity or sexual content, including disguised spellings.
  • No trademarks, brand or company names, logos, websites or social handles. For business placement see Sponsors.
  • No other person's full name without their permission.
  • No political or religious messages.

3. Review

Every name is reviewed by the team before printing. We may adjust capitalization to fit the panel. If a name breaks these rules, we reject it and refund you in full, or you can submit a new name.

4. Placement and duration

The team decides panel location, size, font and order of names. Names stay on for the 2026 season. If a panel is damaged, we will replace it as soon as practical.

5. Photo proof

When your name is on, we email you a photo and it shows as "Approved" in your account. We may share photos of the panel on our website and social media.

6. Not a donation

Ride With The Evil One is a purchase, not a tax deductible donation, and it does not make you a sponsor or give you any rights to the team's name or logos.

7. Refunds

Rejected names are refunded in full. Once a name is approved and printed, the purchase is final. See the Refund Policy.
