    FAQ

    Which package fits my business?

    Title Partner puts your name in the team name and the biggest logo on the tractor. Pit Partner covers the shirt sleeve, the trailer, the website and monthly social posts. Crew Supporter is built for local shops and fans. Not sure? Pick "Not sure" in the form and we'll help.

    Where does The Evil One pull?

    About 20 pulls per season on the NTPA and PPL Badger State circuits, in 10 states. No exhibitions: every time it hits the track, it's there to pull. Dates go up on the Schedule.

    How long does a package run?

    Packages are built around the pulling season. We confirm the exact dates and terms in writing with you before anything goes on the tractor.

    What if the package I want is sold out?

    Hit "Join the waitlist" on that package. We'll contact you if a spot opens up.

    What happens after I send the form?

    We'll email you our sponsor kit within 2 business days.
