FAQ

How long does shipping take?

Every item is printed to order and ships in 5 days. Transit time depends on your address and the shipping method you pick at checkout. See the Shipping Policy.

Can I return or exchange something?

Misprints and defects are replaced free: send us your order number and a photo within XX days. Because everything is printed to order, we can't take returns for size, so please check the size guide first. Full rules in the Refund Policy.

How do the sizes run?

Each product page has a size guide with measurements. Adult tees run S to 3XL and kids run YS to YXL.

What is Ride With The Evil One?

Your name on the tractor for the season. You type the name at checkout and we confirm it before it goes on. Nothing ships. See Ride With The Evil One.

How do I sponsor the team?

There are three packages: Title Partner ($25,000), Pit Partner ($10,000) and Crew Supporter ($5,000). See what each includes on Sponsors, or pick "Sponsorship" above.

Where can I see it pull?

The Evil One runs Pro Stock on the NTPA and PPL Badger State circuits. 2026 dates are coming soon on the Schedule, where you can also get Hook Alerts.
