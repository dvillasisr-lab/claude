012014 · Tomah, WI

The name was never up for debate

It started in 2014 in Tomah, Wisconsin, with one idea.

Chris had always been a heavy metal fan, so when he decided to build a pulling tractor, the name was never up for debate. That idea became a Pro Stock tractor. And that tractor was nicknamed The Evil One.

02February 2020 · First bolt

Twelve years in the making

Heavy Metal wasn't built overnight. The first bolt went in in February 2020.

Then came nights and weekends stolen from work and family. Sketches redrawn more times than anyone can count. A shop in Waterloo, Wisconsin that the whole family turned into a second home, because building Heavy Metal was never a solo job.

03The shop · Waterloo, WI

Nothing off the shelf

What makes Heavy Metal different is under the hood: a Caterpillar 3208 V8, reverse flow, a setup you won't find on any other tractor on the track.

Stock, it measures 636 cubic inches. Ours doesn't: we built it out to 680. That's the whole philosophy behind this build. Nearly everything on Heavy Metal is custom-built. Nothing about it is off the shelf, and nothing about it was quick.

042026 · The night it fired

Then, in 2026, the moment came. Chris stood in the shop and turned the engine over for the first time.

At that exact moment, lightning struck, and the power went out.

Some would call it a coincidence. We call it a sign. From that night on, there was no doubt about what we had built.

052026 · First pull

The pull didn't finish that day

The first pull didn't go the way anyone hoped. A small, unexpected issue came up and the run ended before it should have. It was a disappointing day.

We won't sugarcoat it.

But getting Heavy Metal onto a real track at all, after twelve years of building it with our own hands, was already something to be proud of. The story was just getting started.
