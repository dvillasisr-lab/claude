Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Heavy Metal Pro Stock ("Heavy Metal", "we", "us") collects through this store, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Heavy Metal Pro Stock is a tractor pulling team based in Waterloo, Wisconsin, USA. This store sells team merch that is printed to order and shares sponsorship information. Our store runs on Shopify.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Order details: name, email, phone, shipping and billing address, items purchased.
  • Hook Alerts sign ups: your email address.
  • Sponsorship and contact requests: your name, company, email, phone and message.
  • Account information if you sign in to a customer account.

Information collected automatically: device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, referring site and how you use the store, collected with cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).

Payment information is processed by Shopify and its payment partners. We never see or store your full card number.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, print, ship and support your orders, including returns and replacements.
  • To send Hook Alerts by email: new merch drops and a heads up before each pull. Every email has an unsubscribe link and our mailing address.
  • To answer sponsorship requests and send the media kit you asked for.
  • To keep the store secure, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To understand how the store is used and, only with your consent where required, to measure ads.

4. Who we share it with

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and processes payments.
  • Our print on demand partner (Printly), which receives your name, shipping address and the items ordered so it can print and ship them.
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your order and provide tracking.
  • Our email provider [name pending], to send Hook Alerts.
  • Analytics and advertising partners, only if you allow analytics or marketing cookies.
  • Authorities or others when required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

5. Sale and sharing under state law

If you allow marketing cookies, some ad tools may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information for cross context behavioral advertising under California and other state laws. You can opt out at any time on Your Privacy Choices. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a valid opt out.

6. Cookies

We use necessary cookies to run the store (cart, checkout, security). Analytics, marketing and preference cookies are optional. See how they work and change your choice anytime in Cookie Preferences.

6a. Marketing emails (CAN-SPAM)

We only send Hook Alerts to people who sign up. Every email says who it is from, has a clear subject, includes our mailing address (P.O. Box 42, Waterloo, WI 53594) and a one click unsubscribe link. We process unsubscribes within 10 business days.

7. Your California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose.
  • Delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights.

We do not collect sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit. To make a request, email us (see section 11). We will verify your request by matching it to information we already have, and you may use an authorized agent. Residents of other states with privacy laws can make the same requests.

8. Retention

We keep order records as long as needed for taxes, accounting and warranty claims. Hook Alerts email data is kept until you unsubscribe. Sponsorship requests are kept while we are in contact and then deleted.

9. Children

This store is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Security and changes

We use Shopify's security safeguards, but no method of transmission is 100% secure. We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.

11. Contact

Heavy Metal Pro Stock, P.O. Box 42, Waterloo, WI 53594, USA. Email: [email pending].

1. Misprints, defects and damage

If your item arrives misprinted, defective, damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo of the problem. We will send a free replacement. If the item is no longer available, we refund you in full. You do not need to send the item back unless we ask.

2. Size and change of mind

Because items are printed to order, we do not accept returns or exchanges for the wrong size, color choice or change of mind. Please check the size guide on each product page before you order.

3. Cancellations

Orders go to print quickly. If you need to cancel, email us as soon as possible. If printing has not started, we cancel and refund in full. Once printing starts we cannot cancel.

4. Lost packages

If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or shows delivered but you did not receive it, contact us and we will work with the carrier on a replacement or refund.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to post it.

6. How to start a claim

Email [email pending] with your order number and photos, or use the contact form. We reply within 2 business days.

Accessibility

Last updated: October 1, 2026

We want every fan to be able to shop this store, read about the team and follow the season.

1. Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. That includes keyboard navigation, visible focus, text alternatives for images, enough color contrast, and respecting reduced motion settings.

2. What we are doing

  • Reviewing pages when we add new products or sections.
  • Writing alt text for team and tractor photos.
  • Letting you pause any moving content.

3. Known limits

Some content comes from third parties, such as embedded videos, checkout and payment screens, which we do not fully control.
