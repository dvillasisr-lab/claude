Every item is printed just for you when you order it. That is why we replace anything we got wrong, but we cannot take back items for size or change of mind.

  • Misprints and defects: Replaced free within 30 days
  • Wrong size or change of mind: No returns
  • Lost package: Replaced or refunded
  • Damaged in transit: Replaced free within 30 days

1. Misprints, defects and damage

If your item arrives misprinted, defective, damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo of the problem. We will send a free replacement. If the item is no longer available, we refund you in full. You do not need to send the item back unless we ask.

2. Size and change of mind

Because items are printed when you order them, we do not accept returns or exchanges for the wrong size, color choice or change of mind. Please check the size guide on each product page before you order.

3. Cancellations

Orders go to print quickly. If you need to cancel, email us as soon as possible. If printing has not started, we cancel and refund in full. Once printing starts we cannot cancel.

4. Lost packages

If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or shows delivered but you did not receive it, contact us and we will work with the carrier on a replacement or refund.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to post it.

6. How to start a claim

Email heavymetalevil72@gmail.com with your order number and photos, call (920) 650-4374, or use the contact form. We reply within 2 business days.

7. The Black Smoke Guarantee

If your item ever stops being evil, we replace it. In plain words, this is a limited guarantee against defects:

  • Covered: the print cracking, peeling or fading, and seams or stitching coming apart, within 12 months of delivery, with normal use and following the care label.
  • Not covered: normal wear and tear, the wrong size, damage from misuse, heat, bleach or accidents, and lost or stolen items (see section 4 for lost packages).
  • What you get: a replacement of the same item. If it is no longer available, a similar item or store credit for what you paid.
  • How to claim: use the contact form with your order number and a photo. One replacement per item.

This guarantee is in addition to your rights under law and does not limit them.
