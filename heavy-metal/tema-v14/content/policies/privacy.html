This policy explains what personal information Heavy Metal Pro Stock: “The Evil One” (“we”, “us”) collects through this store, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Heavy Metal Pro Stock: “The Evil One” is a tractor pulling team based in Waterloo, Wisconsin, USA. This store sells team merch that is printed when you order it and shares sponsorship information. Our store runs on Shopify.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Order details: name, email, phone, shipping and billing address, items purchased.
  • Sign-ups for The Evil List: your email address.
  • “Notify me” requests: your email and the item you asked about, so we can tell you when it drops or is back.
  • Sponsorship and contact requests: your name, company, email, phone and message.
  • Account information if you sign in to a customer account.

Information collected automatically: device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, referring site and how you use the store, collected with cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).

Payment information is processed by Shopify and its payment partners. We never see or store your full card number.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, print, ship and support your orders, including returns and replacements.
  • To send The Evil List emails: new merch drops and a heads-up before each pull. Every email has an unsubscribe link and our mailing address.
  • To email you once when an item you asked about with “Notify me” drops or is back in stock.
  • To answer sponsorship requests.
  • To keep the store secure, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To understand how the store is used and, only with your consent where required, to measure ads.

4. Who we share it with

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and processes payments.
  • Our print partner, which receives your name, shipping address and the items ordered so it can print and ship them, and adds the tracking number to your order.
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver your order and provide tracking.
  • Shopify Email, our email provider, to send The Evil List.
  • Analytics and advertising partners, only if you allow analytics or marketing cookies.
  • Authorities or others when required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

5. Sale and sharing under state law

If you allow marketing cookies, some ad tools may count as “selling” or “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under California and other state laws. You can opt out at any time on Your Privacy Choices. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a valid opt-out.

6. Cookies

We use necessary cookies to run the store (cart, checkout, security). Analytics, marketing and preference cookies are optional. See how they work and change your choice anytime in Cookie Preferences.

We only send The Evil List to people who sign up. Every email says who it is from, has a clear subject, includes our mailing address (P.O. 8. Your California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose.
  • Delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for using these rights.

We do not collect sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit. To make a request, email us (see section 12). We will verify your request by matching it to information we already have, and you may use an authorized agent. Residents of other states with privacy laws can make the same requests.

9. Retention

We keep order records as long as needed for taxes, accounting and warranty claims. Your email for The Evil List is kept until you unsubscribe. Sponsorship requests are kept while we are in contact and then deleted.

10. Children

This store is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Security and changes

We use Shopify’s security safeguards, but no method of transmission is 100% secure. We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
