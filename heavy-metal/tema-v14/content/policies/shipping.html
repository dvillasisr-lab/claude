1. Processing time

Every item is printed when you order it. Orders usually ship within 5 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays start processing the next business day.

2. Delivery time

After your order ships, delivery usually takes X to X business days depending on your address and the carrier.

3. Where we ship

To start, we ship to addresses in the United States only, including P.O. boxes where the carrier allows. We do not ship internationally yet.

4. Shipping cost

Shipping is calculated at checkout. US orders over $XX ship free.

5. Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also see it in your account > Orders, or open the order status page from that email. No account needed.

6. Delays

If we cannot ship within the time we promised, we will let you know with a new estimate and give you the option to cancel for a full refund.

7. Address problems

Please check your address at checkout. If a package is returned because of an incorrect address, we can reship it once you cover the new shipping cost.

8. Split shipments

Items in the same order may be printed in different locations and arrive in separate packages.
