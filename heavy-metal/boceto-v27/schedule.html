First time at a pull?

  1. What is Pro Stock

    A diesel class with limits on engine and turbo. “The Evil One” runs a Cat 3208 V8, 680 CI, at 10,000 lb.

  2. What is a full pull

    The sled gets harder to drag the farther it goes. Reach the end of the track and it’s a full pull.

  3. Competition or exhibition

    A competition counts for the class standings. An exhibition is a run for the crowd, no points on the line.

  4. Arrive early

    Grandstands fill before the first class. Come early for a good seat and a look at the pits.

  5. Bring ear protection

    Pro Stock tractors are loud. Ear plugs or muffs for everyone, kids first.

Solo para la dueña · no se ve en el sitio

El calendario NO se edita aparte: se genera solo desde las entradas de Contenido > Metaobjetos > Pull event. ¿Un error? Abre esa entrada, corrige fecha, nombre o tipo y guarda: calendario, lista, historial, contadores y .ics se actualizan solos. Status “Hidden” lo esconde sin borrarlo. Cada pull con Status Done liga solo a su evento en la Galería (“Photos & video”). Al guardar, cambia también “Last updated” en Personalizar > Schedule.
