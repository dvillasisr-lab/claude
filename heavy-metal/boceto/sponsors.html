Why pulling · why this tractor

A story your customers already talk about

  1. The only Cat V8 in the field

    Heavy Metal runs a Caterpillar 3208 V8 built to 680 cubic inches in a Pro Stock class full of John Deere inline sixes. For a Cat dealer, that is not a logo placement. That is the headline.

  2. Seen on the stream, not just the grandstand

    Every hook is a chance for your logo to be on camera.

    Per the leagues and broadcasters: NTPA events stream live on Full Pull Live. PPL events air on Pro Pulling TV and MAVTV.

  3. Matte black makes logos read

    The matte black Challenger body stands out on any track, and a clean dark canvas gives your colors room. No clutter, no fighting for attention.

  4. A full season on the circuit

    About 20 events planned across the NTPA and PPL Badger State circuits in Wisconsin and the Midwest. We are in testing season now, so partners who sign early help shape the schedule.

Sponsor FAQ

Straight answers

Where can my decal go under league rules?

NTPA and PPL Badger State reserve some areas for class, number and league sponsor decals. Every zone on the map above is checked against those rules before we confirm it, and we produce and apply the decals ourselves.

How long is a sponsorship contract?

Standard agreements run one season. Multi-season terms are available and lock in your zone and category. Everything is in writing before a decal goes on.

Can we sponsor with parts, fuel or service instead of cash?

Yes. Pit Crew partnerships are built for in-kind or mixed support, and in-kind value can count toward any tier. We state the value in the contract.

Which events will the tractor run?

About 20 events are planned across the NTPA and PPL Badger State circuits in Wisconsin and the Midwest. We are in testing season, so the confirmed schedule goes to partners first and is posted on the Schedule page.

What reporting do we get?

A one-page report after every pull you are part of, plus a season recap for Full Pull and quarterly reports for Hook partners. See the sample above.
