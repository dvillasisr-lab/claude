The story behind the design

Struck by lightning. Printed on cotton.

The first bolt went into the Evil One in February 2020, in a family shop in Waterloo, Wisconsin. Years of nights and weekends later, in 2026, the Cat 3208 fired for the first time. Lightning struck. The power went out.

Some would call it a coincidence. We call it a sign.

That bolt is on the back of this tee, drawn by the team that built the machine.
