Chapter 01 · Tomah, WI2014

It started with an idea

In 2014, in Tomah, Wisconsin, Chris F. decided to build a pulling tractor. He had always been a heavy metal fan, so the name was never up for debate.

The tractor would be called Heavy Metal. The nickname came later, and it stuck: The Evil One.

Chapter 02 · February2020

The first bolt

In February 2020 the first bolt went in, in the family shop in Waterloo, Wisconsin. From there, almost everything was built by hand.

Nights and weekends. Parts made, fitted, pulled off and made again. The whole family turned that shop into a second home, because building Heavy Metal was never a solo job.

Chapter 03 · 2026

First fire

In 2026 Chris stood in the shop and turned the engine over for the first time. At that exact moment, lightning struck and the power went out.

Some would call it a coincidence. We call it a sign.

Chapter 04 · 2026Pull 1

The first pull ended early

The first pull did not go the way anyone hoped. A small issue came up and the run ended before it should have. We will not sugarcoat it.

But getting Heavy Metal onto a real track at all, after building it with our own hands, was already something to be proud of. The pull did not finish that day. The story was just getting started.

Chapter 07 · Friends on the circuit

Brothers across the ocean

Heavy Metal has a brother in Europe: Diesel Ross, a Pro Stock tractor owned by Gert Stessens from Belgium.

We met Gert at a pull in the Netherlands. Between the look of the two tractors and the friendship between Gert and Chris, Heavy Metal and Diesel Ross are tractor brothers.
