What happens next

Three steps between now and the first full pull.

  1. 01

    Test and fix

    We run the tractor, find what breaks and fix it in the shop in Waterloo.

  2. 02

    Confirm the pulls

    Once the tractor is ready, we lock in pulls on the NTPA and PPL Badger State circuits and post them here.

  3. 03

    Hook Alerts go out

    On pull day you get one message when we hook to the sled, with the stream link when there is one.
