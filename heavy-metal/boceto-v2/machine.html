FAQ

Questions from the fence line

Short answers you can repeat at the pits.

What is Pro Stock?

Pro Stock is a tractor pulling class for heavily modified diesel farm tractors. Each tractor drags a weight transfer sled down a dirt track; the farthest pull wins, and reaching the end of the track is a full pull. Heavy Metal runs Pro Stock at 10,000 lb in NTPA and PPL Badger State.

Why a Cat?

Because nobody else runs one. The class is dominated by John Deere inline sixes; Heavy Metal runs a Caterpillar 3208 V8, reverse flow, built out from 636 to 680 cubic inches. It is the only Cat V8 in the field.

Who built it?

A family shop in Waterloo, Wisconsin. The first bolt went in in February 2020, and almost every part has been made by hand since.

Where can I see it pull?

At NTPA and PPL Badger State events. This is a testing season, so there is no confirmed schedule yet. Get Hook Alerts and we will tell you before we hook.
