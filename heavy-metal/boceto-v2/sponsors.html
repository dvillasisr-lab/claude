Sponsor FAQ

Straight answers

Where can my decal go under league rules?

NTPA and PPL Badger State reserve some areas for class, number and league sponsor decals. Every zone on the map is checked against those rules before we confirm it, and we produce and apply the decals ourselves.

How long is a sponsorship agreement?

Standard agreements run one season. Multi-season terms are available and lock in your zone and category. Everything is in writing before a decal goes on.

Can we sponsor with parts, fuel or service instead of cash?

Yes. Pit Crew partnerships are built for in-kind or mixed support, and in-kind value can count toward any tier. We state the value in the agreement.

Which events will the tractor run?

About 20 events are planned across NTPA and PPL Badger State in Wisconsin and the Midwest. We are in testing season, so the confirmed schedule goes to partners first and is posted on the Schedule page.

What reporting do we get?

A one-page report after every pull you are part of, plus a season recap for Full Pull and quarterly reports for Hook partners.
