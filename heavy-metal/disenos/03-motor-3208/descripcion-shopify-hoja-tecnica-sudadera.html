Engine Spec Sheet No. 3,208. One engine. Eight numbered parts. Zero secrets, except the horsepower, which is still classified.

This is “The Evil One”’s 3208-V8 Reverse Flow, drawn as a technical sheet and printed on a crewneck sweatshirt: turbocharger, compressor inlet, charge pipe, exhaust headers, valve cover, injection lines, breather tube and the billet HEAVY METAL plate, each one called out like a patent drawing. Below it, the turbo inlet and the V8 cover as FIG. 2 and FIG. 3, a spec table, and the signature of the man who built it.

Who it’s for

People who open the hood before they look at the paint. People who can name every part on this shirt. People who can’t, but want to look like they can.

The specs (for people who read manuals)

- Unisex crewneck sweatshirt, classic fit.

- Sizes S to 3XL.

- Warm enough for the late pulls. Not warm enough for the exhaust, so stay back.

- Printed on demand, just for you. No warehouse full of sad leftover sweatshirts.

Care instructions (read these or the sweatshirt gets angry)

- Machine wash cold, inside out.

- Non-chlorine bleach only.

- Tumble dry low.

- Do not iron the print. The ink is fine lines, treat it like a blueprint.

Side effects may include reading the whole shirt while someone is talking to you, and pointing at part number 07 to explain reverse flow. You were warned.
