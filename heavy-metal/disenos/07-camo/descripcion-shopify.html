Camouflage. Ironic, really. This shirt was designed to blend in. Then we put a 3208 V8 on the front and a tractor with glowing eyes, so that plan is dead.

Same “The Evil One” art as the black tee, printed on a black panel so every spark and lightning bolt stays sharp against the camo. Back: HEAVY METAL in big white letters. Sleeves: a warning label, because someone had to.

Pick your terrain

- Desert Camo. For dusty tracks and dirt in places dirt should not be.

- Military Camo. For the pits, the shop, and pretending you can't hear your name being called.

The specs (for people who read manuals)

- Camo tee, unisex fit.

- Front, back, and sleeve prints.

- Printed on demand, just for you. No warehouse full of sad leftover shirts.

Care instructions (read these or the shirt gets angry)

- Machine wash cold, inside out.

- Non-chlorine bleach only.

- Tumble dry low.

- Do not iron the print. It bites.

Side effects may include black smoke, unsolicited conversations about torque, and hunters asking where you got it. Tell them. We need the money for diesel.
