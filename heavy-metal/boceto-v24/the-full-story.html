Twelve years in the making

One idea in 2014. A family shop, nights and weekends, and the night the engine fired and the sky answered. This is how Heavy Metal Pro Stock: “The Evil One” came to be, in our own words.

  • 3 min read
  • Back to Our Story

It started in 2014 in Tomah, Wisconsin, with one idea. Chris had always been a heavy metal fan, so when he decided to build a pulling tractor, the name was never up for debate. That idea became a Pro Stock tractor. And that tractor was nicknamed “The Evil One”.

“The Evil One” wasn't built overnight. The idea was born in 2014, and it took twelve years to turn it into a running machine, because when almost everything on a tractor is custom built, nothing about it is quick.

In February 2020, the first bolt went in at the family shop in Waterloo, Wisconsin. Twelve years of nights and weekends stolen from work and family. Twelve years of sketches redrawn more times than anyone can count. A shop in Waterloo that the whole family turned into a second home, because building “The Evil One” was never a solo job.

The night it fired

Then, in 2026, the moment came. Chris stood in the shop and turned the engine over for the first time. At that exact moment, lightning struck, and the power went out.

“This thing is f#ck!ng evil.”

Chris, owner & driver

Some would call it a coincidence. We call it a sign. From that night on, there was no doubt about what we had built.

The first pull

The first pull didn't go the way anyone hoped. A small, unexpected issue came up and the run ended before it should have. It was a disappointing day. We won't sugarcoat it.

But getting “The Evil One” onto a real track at all, after twelve years of building it with our own hands, was already something to be proud of. The pull didn't finish that day. The story was just getting started.

Nothing off the shelf

What makes “The Evil One” different is under the hood: a Caterpillar 3208 V8, reverse flow, a setup you won't find on any other tractor on the track. Stock, it measures 636 cubic inches. Ours doesn't: we built it out to 680.

And that's really the whole philosophy behind this build. Nearly everything on “The Evil One” is custom-built. Nothing about it is off the shelf, and nothing about it was quick.

Chasing our first full pull

This season, we're chasing our first full pull: about 20 competitions on the NTPA and PPL Badger State circuits. Every time it hits the track, it's there to pull.

Tractor brothers

“The Evil One” has a little brother across the ocean: Diesel Ross, a Pro Stock tractor in Europe owned by Gert Stessens from Belgium. We met Gert at a pull in the Netherlands, and between the look of the two tractors and the friendship between Gert and Chris, “The Evil One” and Diesel Ross are considered tractor brothers.

Our walk-up song: Fear of the Dark, by Iron Maiden.
