Orders

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. If you later sign in with the same email, your past orders show up in your account.

Where is my order confirmation?

We email it right after checkout. If you can't find it, check your spam or promotions folder, then sign in to your account to see the order. Still nothing? Message us with the email you used.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Every item is printed to order, so message us as soon as possible with your order number: pick "Order help" on the contact form. Once an order goes to print we can't change it, so the sooner you reach us the better. Fastest: email heavymetalevil72@gmail.com or call (920) 650-4374.

How do I reach you?

Email heavymetalevil72@gmail.com, call (920) 650-4374 or use the contact form. We reply within 2 business days. Mail goes to P.O. Box 42, Waterloo, WI 53594.

I entered the wrong shipping address. What now?

Message us right away with your order number and the correct address. If the order hasn't shipped, we update it. If it already shipped, we'll work with the carrier, but we can't promise a reroute.

Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Printed to order, ships in 5 days. Transit time depends on your address and the method you choose at checkout. Details in the Shipping Policy.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is calculated at checkout. US orders over $XX ship free.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not right now. We ship to US addresses only.

How do I track my order?

Our print partner, Printify, adds the tracking number to your order the moment it ships. You get a shipping email with the tracking link right away.

You don't need an account: open the order status page from that email, or go to Account > Orders and use the email you ordered with.

Who ships my order?

Printify, our print on demand partner, prints your item and hands it to the carrier (usually USPS, UPS or FedEx, depending on the item and your address). The carrier and tracking number are in your shipping email.

My tracking hasn't updated. Is something wrong?

A label is often created before the carrier scans the package, so tracking can sit still for a bit. If it still hasn't moved after a few business days, message us with your order number and we'll check with Printify and the carrier.

My package says delivered, but I don't have it.

Check around your door, mailbox and with neighbors, and give it one more day: carriers sometimes mark a package delivered early. If it still hasn't shown up, message us with your order number and we'll help sort it out with the carrier.

Will all my items arrive together?

Not always. Different items can be printed at different Printify facilities, so they may ship in separate packages. Each package gets its own tracking email.

Returns & exchanges

Can I return or exchange an item?

Everything is printed to order, so returns work differently than a regular store. What's covered, and for how long, is in the Refund Policy.

My item arrived damaged or misprinted.

Sorry about that. Send us your order number and a photo of the problem: pick "Returns & exchanges" on the contact form. Next steps follow the Refund Policy.

I ordered the wrong size.

Check the Refund Policy for what we can do. Next time, compare the Size guide with a shirt you own before ordering.

How do I start a return or exchange?

Message us with your order number on the contact form. Please don't mail anything back before we reply.

Sizing & fit

How do the sizes run?

Unisex fit. Between sizes? Size up for a relaxed fit. Measurements for every style are in the Size guide.

What sizes do you carry?

Adult tees, long sleeves, hoodies and crewnecks come in S to 3XL. Kids styles come in YS to YXL. Caps and beanies are one size.

How do I measure myself?

The easiest way is to lay a shirt you like flat and measure it. The Size guide shows where to measure chest, length and sleeve.

Will it shrink?

Cotton can shrink a little in a hot wash or dryer. Wash cold and hang dry to keep the fit. Fabric details for each style are on its product page.

Products & care

How do I wash printed merch?
  • Turn it inside out and wash cold.
  • Hang dry.
  • Don't iron over the print and skip bleach.

That keeps the print sharp for more seasons.

What are the shirts made of?

Fabric and weight depend on the style. Each product page lists them, as given by our print partner, Printify.

Who prints the merch?

Printify, our print on demand partner, prints every item when you order it. That's why nothing sits in a warehouse and why orders take a few days to ship.

The color looks different from the picture.

Screens show color differently, so the printed item can look slightly different. If it looks clearly wrong, send us a photo.

A size is sold out. Will it come back?

Leave your email on the product page under the sold out size and we'll email you when it's back.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?

Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and major credit and debit cards, including Visa and Mastercard.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Checkout runs on Shopify. Payments are processed by Shopify and its payment partners, and we never see or store your full card number.

Do you charge sales tax?

Sales tax depends on your address and is calculated at checkout. Prices are in US dollars.

Account

How does sign in work? There's no password.

Right, no password to remember. Enter your email on the Account page, we email you a 6 digit code, and you type it in to sign in.

I didn't get my sign in code.

Give it a minute and check spam or promotions. Make sure the email is spelled right, then request a new code and use the newest one.

I checked out as a guest. Can I see my orders?

Yes. Sign in with the same email you used at checkout and your past orders show up.

How do I update my saved address?

Sign in, open Addresses in your account and edit or add one. For an order already placed, see "I entered the wrong shipping address" above.

How do I delete my account or data?

Send a request through the contact form. How we handle it is in the Privacy Policy.

Our one email list: new merch drops and a heads up before every pull. No spam.

How do I sign up?

Enter your email in the Hook Alerts box at the bottom of any page or on the Schedule. Hook Alerts are email only.

How do I unsubscribe?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any Hook Alerts email, or turn it off in your account.

Special edition

What is a Special edition?

A short run of merch made for one moment of the season, like the night the engine first fired and the lightning struck. It has its own design and lives on its own page, apart from the regular shop. See the current one on Special edition.

Are Special edition pieces numbered?

Yes. Each run is limited to XX pieces and every piece is numbered. Numbers are assigned in order as orders come in.

When is there a new one?

There's no fixed calendar. A Special edition drops around a big moment of the season. Hook Alerts get the heads up first: sign up at the bottom of any page.

Will a Special edition come back?

No. When the run sells out, it's gone. Regular designs stay in the shop.
