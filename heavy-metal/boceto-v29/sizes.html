How to measure

Measure a shirt you already like, laid flat

  1. 1Lay it flat. Use a shirt or hoodie that fits the way you like. Smooth out wrinkles, sleeves to the side.
  2. AChest width. Straight across, armpit to armpit.
  3. BBody length. From the highest point of the shoulder, next to the collar, down to the hem.
  4. CSleeve. From the shoulder seam to the end of the cuff.
  5. 2Compare. Find the size in the chart closest to your numbers. Between sizes? Size up for a relaxed fit.

Hats: measure around your head, just above the ears and eyebrows. Printed-to-order items can’t be returned for size, so it’s worth the two minutes. See the Refund Policy.
