En Shopify esto es un producto "Gift card" nativo (sin apps) con 3 variantes: $25, $50 y $100. Usa la plantilla product.gift-card. El formulario "Email it to them" es el de destinatario que ya trae Shopify: correo, nombre, mensaje de 200 caracteres y fecha de envío de hasta 90 días. Los montos se cambian en el producto, no en el tema. El link en el menú, en el footer y en el carrito vacío sale de Navegación.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick an amount

    $25, $50 or $100. Add it to the cart with anything else or check out on its own.

  2. 2

    We email it

    To you, so you can print it, or straight to them on the day you choose, with your note.

  3. 3

    They gear up

    They enter the code at checkout. Whatever they don't spend stays on the card.

Gift card questions

Anything else? See the FAQ or send us a note.

Does it expire?

No. The balance stays on the card until it's used up.

What can they buy with it?

Anything in the shop, special edition included. It can't be used to buy another gift card.

Can I schedule it for a birthday?

Yes. Pick “Email it to them”, choose the date and we send it that day. You get a copy of the receipt right away.

How do they check the balance?

The link in the gift card email always shows what's left. Their balance also shows at checkout when they enter the code.

They lost the email. Now what?

Send us a note with your order number and we'll send it again.

Estas preguntas salen de metaobjetos FAQ item con la página "gift" (se agrega esa opción al campo pages). También aparecen en el FAQ general, en la categoría Orders. Que no venza es un ajuste de Shopify: Configuración > Tarjetas de regalo > Vencimiento: "Las tarjetas de regalo nunca vencen".
