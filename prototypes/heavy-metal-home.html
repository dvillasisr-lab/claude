  1. We don't race the clock.

    A pull is one run against a sled that gets heavier the farther you drag it. Distance is the score.

  2. Black smoke is the point.

    Diesel, steel and a crowd on the fence. That is the whole show, and we bring it to every county fair that will have us.

    Every order goes back into the tractor. No investors, no factory team. Just the crew and whoever wears the gear.
