Section 2 · What we found

What we found, in three sentences

FINDING
WHAT IT MEANS
WHAT WE DO
1
73% of the people we wrote to (14.2M) did not respond to anything in nine months, and 59% of our messages go through a channel that person has never responded to.
We pay for messages nobody reads and wear out the permission: Marketing gets 5.9 unsubscribes per 1,000 emails, more than double the other teams (2.6).
Send each person through the channel they responded to in the last 90 days; WhatsApp only to people who read one, with 10% held out. MarTech and Data build it; Marketing applies it. Week 3 to Month 2.
2
Messages with a reason win: 12% of our emails, 57% of the clicks (26 vs 3.5 clicks per 1,000). In-app wins 20.7 loans per 1,000 messages with 4% of what we send.
List promotions get ignored or unsubscribed, while the sign-up reminder wins a loan for MXN 21. The message is not the problem; who gets it, and why, is.
Rebuild the 10 biggest list promotions as journeys that fire on something the person did, starting with Tasa 0 to lapsed leads; move referrals into in-app. Marketing with Product Growth, Weeks 1 to 4.
3
Braze has no owner and no shared rules: only 1 of 3,228 Canvases has a real control group, 748 communications have no team or ticket, and there is no limit per person.
Nobody can say what Braze actually sells. Up to 2.3M people a month get three or more campaigns in one day, and 43.3M promotions went to people with a frozen KPay line.
Global exclusions (Week 1), a 10% control group on every promotion (Week 2), and limits of 5 promotions a week and 1 push a day (Month 2). MarTech configures; CRM runs a weekly review.
