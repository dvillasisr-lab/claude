## PRINT PRODUCTION EXPERT. Checks run: opened all six PNGs, the v4 reference and the Facebook reference. Used PyMuPDF on all three cards.pdf files to read physical text sizes, image ppi, vector vs raster, and the trim and bleed boxes. Ran pixel scans for the safe zone and bleed, took 3x crops to look at cutout edges, and measured tractor-to-background luminance.

MAIN FINDING (this affects every candidate and the brief itself): the brief says "11 px ≈ 6.5 pt", and that is wrong by about 2.5x. The canvas is 1125 px for 3.75 in, which is 300 dpi, so 1 px = 0.24 pt. 11 px is really 2.64 pt, and 6.5 pt would be 27 px. The PDFs confirm this. All mono labels in all three designs print at 2.64 to 2.88 pt, and the contact lines print at 3.96 pt (B), 4.32 pt (C) and 4.79 pt (A). Grey type at 2.6 pt knocked out of a 4-colour rich black will fill in and fail to register on any offset press. Some labels are #83888d grey, which makes it worse. None of the three can go to press as is. v4 has the same flaw.

Also common to all three: the PDFs are RGB, TrimBox and BleedBox equal the MediaBox, and there are no crop marks. The 1 px (0.24 pt) rules are drawn at 16 to 30% tints, so they will break up or disappear. All three use the same cutout. It has a faint smoke and fringe wisp beside the stack, and the stack top is blown to paper white. Tractor separation is fine: hood luminance is about 80 against 11 to 33 for the ground, so "muddy" is overstated. B has the weakest separation because its lifted grey background sits at 33.
- **a-editorial** 6/10: The cleanest file for press. The line logo is flat bone-white vector, not raster and not gradient. The ground is flat black apart from the light pool. The tractor is placed at 300 ppi and ends about 1 px inside the safe line (x 1051). It has the best QR: 152 px for 29 modules is 5.24 px per module (0.44 mm), dark on a bone panel, vector. It also has the largest contact type (4.79 pt) and a strong name (18.7 pt). Failures: the micro labels are 2.64 to 2.88 pt, including the yellow 01 to 03 and the grey TEL/MAIL/WEB at #83888d. The QR quiet zone is 20 px, which is 3.8 modules and under the 4-module spec. The yellow finish tick is only 2 px (0.48 pt). None of this is structural. It is a type-scale and rule-weight fix.
